Task 3 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Problem Statement:
Netflix hosts thousands of titles across genres, countries, and ratings. The goal is to explore the dataset to identify content trends, popular genres, and regional patterns.

Dataset:
Name: Netflix Movies and TV Shows
Source: Kaggle
Size: ~8,800 titles

Approach:
Computed summary statistics and checked for null values
Visualized content type distribution (Movies vs TV Shows)
Analyzed ratings, top genres, top countries, and release year trends
Plotted yearly content addition trend to identify growth patterns

Results:
~70% of Netflix content is Movies
USA, India, and UK are the top content-producing countries
International Movies and Dramas are the most common genres
Content additions peaked around 2019
TV-MA is the most frequent rating, indicating a primarily adult audience
